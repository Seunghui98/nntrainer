A bakery sells bread rolls in bags of 6 and bags of 10. On Monday it sold 23 bags and 178 rolls in total. On Tuesday it sold twice as many bags of 6 as on Monday and 4 fewer bags of 10, and each roll costs 35 cents. How many bags of each size were sold on Monday, how many rolls were sold on Tuesday, and how much more or less money did the bakery take on Tuesday than on Monday? Solve it step by step, show each equation, and check your answer at the end.
