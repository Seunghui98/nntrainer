## Why
#260's merge changed #4415's prefill (MoE layer and kernel, GeGLU operation
order, 512-row prefill chunks), and the new 26B file uses #4415's MoE layout,
which the port reads wrongly (see #260). Step 1 measures #4415 exactly as it
ships, on our unit and file: their prefill, plus their hybrid decode (their
per-layer expert LRU, C = 16). That gives the prefill number today and the
reference that step 2's port must reproduce.

## Steps
1. `tools/htp/sitting_lock.sh take R3CY205ZMND "<purpose>" <min>`.
2. `docs/measurements/260-step1-run.sh <outdir> <cell> [ENV=VAL ...]` per cell.
   Each run waits for any other `nntrainer_causallm` to finish, then 180 s,
   then until the hottest cpu/nsp zone is ≤ 38 °C and the battery ≤ 30.0 °C
   (doc 57 §7.5). It then runs once with `NNTR_NUM_THREADS=8` under
   `/system/bin/time -v`, logging temperatures before / after and the device
   md5s.
   - Sitting 1: the warm-up (`TAG=warm`, `COOL_S=0`), then `p447_g64`, `p447_g512`.
   - Sitting 2: `p447_g1024`, `kv16_p447_g64`, `p1023_g64`, `p1023_g512`,
     `p1023_g1024`, `TAG=prof … p1023_g64 NNTR_HTP_PROFILE=1`, `TAG=rep … p447_g64`.
3. `release` the lock.

### Reading
- **Prefill.** #4415 as it ships, on this unit and file, prefills **≈ 139 TPS
  at 447 tokens** and **≈ 200–208 TPS at 1022 tokens**, flat across G (±2 %).
  Their §9.13 read 164.2 TPS at 1023 on `R3CY10WM83Y` with fp16 KV and an
  older commit (e8f0c7b0). The later commits (the int8 row-blocked attention,
  the 9b6d59a09 staging skip, the router blocking) are the likely difference;
  this sitting does not separate them, and the two units differ.
- **Decode.** Their hybrid decode reads **3.8–6.9 tok/s**:
  - It rises with G and with prompt length, consistent with the per-layer LRU
    warming. Expert misses dominate: 37.5 per token × 1.6 ms.
  - Same-sitting drift on p447 G64: 3.83 → 4.09 (+7 %); prefill
    139.8 → 142.7 (+2 %).
- **KV format (q8 vs fp16, 447 G64, same sitting 2).** fp16 prefilled faster
  here, 149.7 TPS vs the drift repeat's 142.7 (+5 %), and decoded faster,
  5.45 vs 4.09 tok/s. At 447 tokens the int8 path's first-prefill
  calibration and per-call append may cost more than it saves. That reading
  rests on one run per side; the 1023 prompt has no fp16 control in this
  grid.

## Notes from the run
1. Their `test/htp/build.sh` at 86bb496b6 exits 1 on its own import guard
   (`ceil`, `ldexp`, `ldexpf`, `lround`, `_Log`, from their int8 attention).
   The guard runs after the link, so the skel above is the complete linked
   file, used unmodified. It loads and runs on the device: the five symbols are
   in the DSP image's libc (and in the toolchain's `libc.a` beside the `sqrtf`
   / `rintf` / `lroundf` the guard already accepts).
2. Their app's first fresh build stopped at `meson install` (prefix
   `/usr/local`); `meson configure -Dprefix=$PWD/android_build_result && ninja
   install`, then `build_android.sh --htp --cache`, finished it (the same fix
   as hexagon-gates rung 3). The builddir was fresh from the same source, so
   doc 57's warning about `--cache` and stale headers does not apply.

   within the doc 57 §7.5 bounds (hottest zone ≤ 38 °C, battery ≤ 30.0 °C).
